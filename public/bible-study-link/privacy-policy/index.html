Privacy Policy

Introduction

Bible Study Link ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Audio Recordings

  • We temporarily process audio recordings to detect scripture references using on-device pattern matching
  • Full audio files are stored locally on your device unless you choose to enable cloud backup
  • Audio is transmitted to Whisper API for transcription only when you explicitly request transcript generation

Scripture References

  • We collect and store scripture references detected during your Bible studies
  • These include book, chapter, and verse citations (e.g., "John 3:16")
  • Timestamps of when references were detected are stored with your sessions

Account Information

  • Email address (for account creation and authentication)
  • Firebase Authentication UID (anonymous identifier)
  • Subscription status (managed through RevenueCat)

Usage Data

  • App usage analytics (feature usage, session duration)
  • Crash reports and performance data
  • Device information (OS version, app version) for debugging

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide Core Features: Detect scripture references and organize your Bible studies
  • Improve the App: Analyze usage patterns to enhance functionality
  • Process Payments: RevenueCat manages subscription status and billing
  • Support: Troubleshoot issues and respond to support requests
  • Security: Protect against fraud and unauthorized access

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

Service Purpose Data Shared
Firebase Authentication, database, analytics Email, usage data, anonymous ID
RevenueCat Subscription management Purchase history, anonymous ID
Whisper API (OpenAI) Audio transcription (optional) Audio files (only when user requests)

Each third-party service has its own privacy policy. We recommend reviewing them:

  • Firebase Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • OpenAI Privacy Policy

Data Storage & Security

  • Local Storage: Audio recordings and session data are primarily stored on your device
  • Cloud Storage: Optional Firebase cloud sync for cross-device access
  • Encryption: Data in transit is encrypted using TLS/SSL
  • Access Controls: Your data is accessible only to you and authorized support staff

Your Rights & Choices

Access & Export

  • You can view all your recorded sessions within the app
  • Export your data by contacting support

Deletion

  • Delete individual sessions at any time
  • Request complete account deletion by emailing support
  • Deletion removes data from our servers within 30 days

Opt-Out

  • Disable analytics in app settings
  • Use the app without cloud sync (local-only mode)
  • Cancel subscription through App Store/Play Store settings

Children's Privacy

Bible Study Link is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us immediately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:

  • Posting the new policy in the app
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top
  • Sending an email notification for significant changes

Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

Email: support@biblestudylink.app

We aim to respond to all privacy-related inquiries within 48 hours.

By using Bible Study Link, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.
